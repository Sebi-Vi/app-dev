### **Plot**
It's a **supernatural/romance** story about a girl and her friendship with a stranged masked man. 
It tells a story about a girl named *Hotaru Takegawa* who got lost in a forest inhabited by a mountain spirit and a yokai.
There she met the mask-wearing, human-like entity named *Gin*, who told *Hotaru* that he will disappear when touched by a human.
As time passes by, *Hotaru* grows up while *Gin* stays the same.
At the time they went to a festival hosted by the spirits as a date, despite *Gin* wanting to hold *Hotaru*, he can't
The night ends in a tragedy as a *Gin* mistakenly touch a young boy who is about to fall down, he starts to disappear.
Before he completely disappears, he and *Hotaru* embrace each other and confess their love.
The story ends with *Hotaru* accepting her pain and moving on with her life.
